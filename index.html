      HOW TO PLAY

      Run the bakery

      Make a cupcake

      Click a station to walk there, then click what to do. The keys shown are optional shortcuts.

      1. Q Ingredients - pick a batter flavor
      2. W Oven - bake it, then take it out before it burns
      3. E Frosting - frost it
      4. R Toppings - add a topping (or leave it plain)
      5. F Packaging - box it for 🎁 to-go orders
      6. D Serving - hand it to the customer

      Optional shortcuts: at a station, 1-9 picks an action. Esc pauses.

      Streaks

      Serve customers back to back to build a streak. Every 3 in a row raises your score multiplier and gives a streak bonus: extra points, and every waiting customer cheers up.

      If a customer runs out of patience and leaves, the streak breaks.

      Secret combos

      Some flavor + frosting + topping combinations are secret recipes worth much more. Find them yourself, or check the Recipe Book station for hints. "Surprise me!" customers take anything, so they're your chance to experiment.

      Heat

      Every station heats up as you use it - especially Turbo bake. When a station overheats (JAM) it jams, and the heat sets off a chain reaction down the line to the next stations. Cool a station by walking to it and clicking Cool it down.

      Days and the shop

      Earn each day's coin goal to open again tomorrow. Spend coins on upgrades and new ingredients between days. Survive all five days to win.

      Bake-Off

      Two players take turns: same budget, same customers. Highest score wins.
