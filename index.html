Welcome to the
Chain Reaction Bakery

One cupcake order sets off a chain reaction: every station's output powers the next. Keep the chain going from the first egg to the final customer - then break it on purpose in the Chain Reaction Lab and watch the damage cascade.

Directions:Click or tap to move Objective:Keep the chain reaction going through all 8 stations.

Made by Tanvi for the Chain Reaction! game jam. One small spark, eight stations, one big reaction :)

HOW IT WORKS

Keep the Chain Going

Each station sets off the next: ingredients start the batter, the batter fills the tray, the oven triggers frosting, and so on to the customer. Finish the chain, then trigger a meltdown in the Chain Reaction Lab.

CHAIN REACTION LAB

Trigger a shock

The bakery is a pipeline: Ingredients feed Batter feed Assembly feed the Oven, which fans out to Frosting and Packaging, on to Decorating and Serving. Shock a station and watch the reaction cascade downstream through the dependency graph, modeled after DebtRank, the algorithm used to measure contagion risk in financial networks.
